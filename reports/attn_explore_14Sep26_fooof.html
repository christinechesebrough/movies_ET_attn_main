Aperiodic and periodic spectral features by gaze state

The same states as entries 12 to 14 of the Attention State Exploration Log, applied to the spectral parameterisation instead of band amplitude. Features come from the knee-mode FOOOF fits over 1 to 57 Hz on the same 10 s window grid (see TODO C0b): the aperiodic exponent, offset and knee frequency, and the periodic power in theta, alpha, beta and gamma after the aperiodic fit is removed. Peak presence and centre-frequency features are left out for now. Values are contact-relative: each contact's mean over all its kept windows is subtracted, so a bar reads as "this contact's exponent in this state minus its own average exponent". The neural analyses use the pipeline's inclusion list of 42 recordings (16 English, 13 Hungarian, 13 Inscapes; the eye-side analyses use 49), and FOOOF files exist for 41 of them, NS205 English being the one not yet extracted.

States use the deviation gate with the within-timepoint internal condition, the third row of entries 13 and 14 there: internal regions require timepoint-relative deviation above 0.6, external regions require within-subject deviation at or below 0, and the six PC1 regions, three collapsed states and planned trend and endpoint contrasts are as before. The two-state comparison uses the conj rule at 0.6 for a like-for-like with entry 12. Tests are the two-stage mixed model throughout; the correction scheme is the one used in entries 13 and 14 of the log.

Trend across the six states in the hypothesis networks

Two-state contrast in the hypothesis networks

Reading, first pass. The aperiodic exponent and offset rise from the external to the internal end in most networks where the trend is significant (Y17: 8 rising, 1 falling for each; Y7: 4 and 0), so the spectrum steepens as the viewer moves toward the internal pole, consistent with the low-frequency amplitude rise in entry 13. The knee frequency trends upward where significant but its region profile is not monotone. Periodic alpha rises toward the internal end (8 rising, 0 falling on Y17) and is the only periodic feature with a consistent trend; periodic theta, beta and gamma are near zero on this scale. The two-state contrast is weaker than the trend: exponent and offset are significant in a minority of networks with mixed signs across films. The dip in the gated internal band for exponent and offset, below both neighbours, is worth a look before this is read further.

Theta and alpha peak presence, percent of windows

For each contact and state, the percentage of that state's windows in which the FOOOF fit placed a peak in the theta or alpha range. Unlike the features above these are not contact-centred: a bar reads as the raw percentage. Contrasts are still per-contact differences tested with the mixed model. Y17 and Y7 networks; the per-network figures show only rows with a significant result in at least one film. Recordings with FOOOF files in the inclusion list: 15 English, 13 Hungarian, 13 Inscapes.
